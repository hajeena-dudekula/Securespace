# SecureSpace - Problem Statement

## Problem

Traditional document-sharing systems often have security and access control issues.

Confidential documents may be accessed by unauthorized users due to incorrect permissions, shared links, or weak access control.

Organizations may also struggle to track who accessed, downloaded, modified, or deleted a document.

When a security incident occurs, it can be difficult to identify who accessed the document and when the activity happened.

## Proposed Solution

SecureSpace is a secure document-sharing platform designed to protect confidential documents through controlled access and a real audit trail.

The platform allows users to upload, organize, and share documents while ensuring that only authorized users can access them.

It also records document activities to provide accountability and help administrators monitor document access.

## Project Goal

To develop a secure, user-friendly document-sharing application that provides controlled document access, permission management, and activity tracking.
